لماذا

  • كل تطبيق فحصته إما يكتب الاسم بخطوط جاهزة، أو يولّد صوراً عامة بنماذج لا تذكر العربية في وثائقها.
  • لم أجد أي مثال رسمي منشور لاسم عربي مولَّد داخل أي تطبيق.
  • التطبيق لا يكون أدق من النموذج الذي يستخدمه، لذلك خطأ «مدى» الذي ظهر مثل «م م ى» قد يتكرر داخل أي تطبيق.
  • أقوى ادعاءات رسمية للعربية جاءت من نموذجين خارج متجر التطبيقات، ولم أختبرها.
  • لم أجرّب أي عرض تجريبي، لأن هذه الجلسة بلا متصفح تفاعلي، والعروض تحتاج متصفحاً أو رفع صورة أو حساباً.

7 · Can letter fidelity be improved? Honest limits

  1. Stronger instructions. Spell letters one by one, state the count, forbid extra letters, marks and tashkeel. Helps; guarantees nothing.
  2. Reference image. Render the exact name correctly shaped and pass it as an input image, asking the model to recompose the same letters. This gives the true letter skeleton. OpenAI masks are approximate and gpt-image-2 ignores input_fidelity; Gemini 3.1 Flash takes up to 10 object references. Effect on Arabic accuracy U until tested.
  3. Separate Arabic check. Google Vision reads printed Arabic, not handwriting (about 1.50 USD per 1,000 after 1,000 free a month). Azure Read: printed only. AWS Textract: no Arabic. Tesseract, EasyOCR and Qari-OCR: printed only. None claims calligraphy. A vision-language checker's accuracy on interlaced calligraphy is U. Any checker must first be measured on a labelled in-house set that includes known failures; each rejected image is still paid for, raising cost per visible design and adding latency.
  4. Human check before production stays necessary whatever the model.

A bespoke vector composition engine is possible but is a large project, not a quick guarantee. Not recommended now.

9 · Ranking: three candidates worth a real trial

  1. 1 · Model bake-off with a reference image (not an app) The failure the owner saw is letter accuracy. Every app delegates to a general model and none documents Arabic. A bake-off answers the real question for a few riyals and applies to any host. Compares gpt-image-2 text-only (baseline), gpt-image-2 with reference, Gemini 3.1 Flash Image with reference (only official Arabic text-in-image listing), and Seedream 5.0 Pro if reachable without a new commercial commitment.
  2. 2 · Cloudlift Live Product Options AI extension Installed; documented shopper text-to-image with name placeholder and style presets; order-line file link; cheapest app route with 1,000 free credits. Risks: unnamed model with fallback to cheaper models; three outputs per request and history unknown; extension prorated at 9–10 USD with no trial.
  3. 3 · Zakeke Only app documenting N images per prompt with the shopper choosing one; Logos mode; mature listing; only Arabic/RTL evidence (text tool only). Risks: most expensive; model unknown. Start with its public demo before any install.

Reserve: FramoAI — its own-key plan could host a bake-off winner, but it has no reviews yet. Not recommended here: Customily (DALL·E 3, no Arabic docs, uncappable 10,000 USD usage cap), Teeinblue (photo only), Zepto (no AI), Qstomizer and SnapArt (SDXL).

10 · Acceptance gates

G1
Exact lettersOwner grades each image: same letters, order and count; correct joins; essential dots; original hamza; no extra letters, marks, tashkeel or frames. Pass: ≥ 2 of 3 in every batch and ≥ 5 of 6 per name.
G2
Three distinct layoutsThree different composition families per batch, comparable to the benchmark's compact, horizontal and looped designs.
G3
Nine visible choicesThree batches yield 9 visible designs; failed or blocked images do not consume them; all 9 selectable after reload and return.
G4
Chosen original on the orderAuthorized test order carries a durable link to the exact file; SHA-256 matches; link works after 7 days.
G5
Bounded costServer-side SAR ceiling per day and in total; measured cost per 100 visible designs ≤ owner target (suggested 30 SAR); no open-ended vendor usage cap.
G6
Usable latencyp90 ≤ 30 s to show 3 designs on a phone over mobile data across ≥ 5 batches; page stays usable.

Current prototype: G3–G6 plumbing demonstrated (proxy, storage, cart, one 3-image batch in 14.5 s, retained designs, durable cart URL). G1–G2 not met (owner rejected output). G4 completed-order proof pending. G3 quota counts possibly billed images, not 9 visible designs. The 8-images-in-two-batches report is unexplained.
